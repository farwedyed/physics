Exam Gold // Why are home appliances connected in Parallel?
1. Every appliance gets the full rated voltage (220 V).
2. Each device operates independently on its own switch (if TV turns off, Refrigerator stays running).
3. Adding more parallel branches reduces total circuit resistance ($R_{eq}$), so each branch draws current without choking the others!
